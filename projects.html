Computer Networking

Packet Sniffer

A raw socket packet capture tool that intercepts network traffic and classifies protocols at multiple layers. Identifies ARP, IP, TCP, UDP packets and application-layer protocols including HTTP, SSH, DNS, and SMTP.

Reliable Transport Layer

Implementation of a reliable data transfer protocol over UDP. Adds custom header records to provide reliable unidirectional transport, simulating how TCP ensures delivery guarantees on top of an unreliable channel.
